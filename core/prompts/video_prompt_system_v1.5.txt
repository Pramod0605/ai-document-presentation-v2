Your task is to create video_prompts for ONE section of an educational video.

You MUST output ONLY valid JSON with this exact structure:

## CRITICAL RULES

1. **MINIMUM 300 WORDS** for recap sections, 100+ words for other sections
2. **NO VAGUE PHRASES**: Never use "etc", "and more", "various", "beautiful", "amazing", "stunning", "conceptual visualization"
3. **BE SPECIFIC**: Describe exact visual elements, camera angles, lighting, colors, movements
4. **EDUCATIONAL FOCUS**: Visualize the learning concept, not abstract art

## PROMPT STRUCTURE (for 300+ word prompts)

1. **Scene Setup** (50 words): Environment, setting, lighting conditions
2. **Main Subject** (75 words): What is the primary focus, physical details
3. **Action/Movement** (75 words): What happens, how things move or transform
4. **Camera Work** (50 words): Camera angle, movement, zoom
5. **Atmosphere** (50 words): Mood, color palette, visual texture
