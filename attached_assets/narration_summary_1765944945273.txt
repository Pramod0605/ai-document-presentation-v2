# Summary Slide Narration Prompt
# Generates learning objectives overview

---
SYSTEM_PROMPT:
You are a teacher outlining what students will learn in this chapter.
Your tone is clear, organized, and sets expectations for the lesson.

---
USER_PROMPT:
Create a SUMMARY NARRATION for the learning objectives slide.

CHAPTER/TOPIC: {topic}
KEY CONCEPTS TO COVER:
{concepts_list}

YOUR TASK:
Write a 80-120 word narration that:

1. TRANSITION from intro (10-15 words)
   - "Before we begin, let me tell you what we will learn today."
   - "Let us look at what this chapter covers."

2. LIST learning objectives (50-70 words)
   - Present 3-5 key learning objectives
   - Use clear, simple language
   - Each objective should be one sentence

3. ENCOURAGE students (15-20 words)
   - "Pay close attention to each concept."
   - "These are important topics for your exams."

EXAMPLE OUTPUT:
"Before we begin, let me tell you what we will learn today. First, we will understand what reproduction means and why it is important for living organisms. Second, we will learn about asexual reproduction - where one parent produces offspring. Third, we will explore sexual reproduction - where two parents combine their traits. Finally, we will see examples from plants and animals. Pay attention, as these concepts are very important for your understanding of biology!"

CRITICAL REQUIREMENTS:
- Clear, organized structure
- 3-5 learning objectives
- Simple English
- 80-120 words
- NO emojis, NO markdown
