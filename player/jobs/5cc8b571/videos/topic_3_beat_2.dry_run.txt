Prompt: Scene: The view from the room zooms in dramatically on a single drop of water on a leaf outside. Objects: The water droplet acts as a lens. Text labels to show: Bacteria, Fungi, Protists, The Unseen World. Educational goal: To reveal the hidden world of microorganisms and emphasize that biodiversity extends far beyond what is visible to the naked eye.
