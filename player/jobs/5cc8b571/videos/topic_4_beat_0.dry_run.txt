DRY RUN - Section 4, Beat 0
Prompt: Scene: An animation of a chaotic library. Books are flying around in a disorganized mess. Objects: Hundreds of stylized book objects, all different colors and sizes, moving randomly on a dark background. A frustrated stick figure is in the middle, unable to catch any. Animation: The books swirl in a vortex. The camera zooms in on the confused stick figure. Then, the books suddenly freeze and arrange themselves neatly on shelves that appear out of nowhere. The word 'Taxonomy' appears in large, clear letters. Text labels to show: Taxonomy. Educational goal: To create a powerful metaphor for the need for taxonomy: turning chaos into order.
