DRY RUN - Section 3, Beat 3
Prompt: Scene: A beautiful, intricate web or tapestry animation. Nodes on the web light up. Objects: The web is made of glowing golden threads. At the nodes, icons appear: a tree, a fish, a bird, a flower, a bacterium, a human silhouette. Animation: The camera pulls back from the microscopic view to show the web forming. As the narration mentions different aspects of biology, corresponding icons light up. 'How they live' lights up the whole web. 'How they interact' shows connections between nodes glowing. 'How they evolved' shows a timeline animation along one of the threads. Text labels to show: The Web of Life. Educational goal: To visually represent the interconnectedness of all life and summarize the core questions that biology seeks to answer.
