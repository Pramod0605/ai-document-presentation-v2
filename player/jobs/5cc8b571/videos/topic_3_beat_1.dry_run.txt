DRY RUN - Section 3, Beat 1
Prompt: Scene: The scene transitions to a typical room interior, looking out a window. Objects: A potted plant on the windowsill, a fluttering butterfly (insect) outside the window, a bird flying in the sky, and a cat (pet) sleeping on a chair. Animation: The camera pans across the room. It focuses for a moment on the potted plant. Then it moves to the window, where the butterfly flutters past. The view then tilts up to see the bird, and finally pans back to the cat sleeping peacefully. The word 'Biodiversity' appears. Text labels to show: Biodiversity. Educational goal: To connect the concept of biodiversity to the student's immediate, observable surroundings.
